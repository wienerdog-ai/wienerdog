- [high] [A] Canonical crash-survival claims exceed the stated fsync guarantee (docs/specs/WP-quarantine-preserve-durability.md:695-699)
  F6 categorically says that any artifact surviving a crash has complete bytes, while F10 says the bytes and inode “do survive” the same-inode-relink scenario. Those claims are stronger than the spec’s own contract at lines 1555–1570 and 2170–2171: the evidence establishes completed platform flush calls, not crash survival or arrival on the medium. A platform/device may preserve the directory entry while failing to persist complete file data, so the F6 implication is not guaranteed either. This is a durability claim beyond what the package says the platform provides, not one of F10’s disclosed race residuals.
  Recommendation: Replace both survival assertions with order-only language: the artifact fsync completed before the directory fsync, and the inode had received a completed platform flush before the relink, without claiming crash survival beyond platform semantics.
