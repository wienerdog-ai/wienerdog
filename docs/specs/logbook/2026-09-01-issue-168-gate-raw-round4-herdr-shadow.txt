  Impact: The seam introduced to prevent exfiltration can silently disable the primary reporting channel elsewhere. Semantic “must say”

  canonical reason, subject, and append policy inside failLoud; never accept caller-provided free-form subjects or skip booleans. Unknown
  values must fail closed, and a skipped append must return false. Assert exact HEAD behavior for undefined and exact canonical strings for
