# A derived predicate is only as complete as its seed, and the seed is a surface nobody registers (2026-07-26)

**A derived predicate is only as complete as its seed, and the seed is a surface
nobody registers (2026-07-26).** Across five adversarial rounds on the two
secret-fence legs, the blocking finding landed on one family three consecutive
times, and the family was never the contract — it was the thing that claimed to
enumerate the contract. Round 3: **M4e**, a hand-maintained register of A8's
clauses, was incomplete. Round 4: **V-15**'s five hand-listed figure pins were
incomplete. Round 5, both legs at once and from opposite directions: leg 1's
sweep was *generated* but filtered through **`FIG_SHAPES`**, a hand-written list
of number shapes, so a real measured figure it omitted drifted silently; and its
clause register counted **`[C]` markers** that a human places by hand, so an
unmarked clause left both counts at nine and the build green. Leg 2's membership
was *generated* too — by **`QSWEEP`**, a hand-written regex whose `[Aa]ny…finding`
alternation is case-sensitive on the first letter. `src/core/dream/validate.js`
writes `ANY` in capitals, so the sweep scored **zero** on the work package's own
centre-of-gravity file and never admitted it to the family it claimed was
complete. Each round replaced a hand-maintained list with a mechanism whose own
input was hand-maintained one level down, **and each round's prose claimed the
regress had ended.**

**The terminating move is subtraction, and it has two forms.** Where the region
is bounded, digest the whole of it and delete the seed: leg 1's restatement
register now hashes 2,894 lines of non-block prose with no vocabulary at all, and
its proof is a probe asserting that *an ordinary sentence stating no figure*
moves the digest — coverage of prose, not of a list of numbers. Where the seed
cannot be deleted because the mechanism genuinely needs one, **register the seed
as canonical contract text**: leg 2 made `QSWEEP` itself a decided artifact,
forbade narrowing it to make the step pass, and required the file set to be
re-derived by execution with a record each time it moves. Both forms buy the same
property — the register can no longer be short without something turning red —
and both cost churn, which is the honest trade and is now stated in the steps
rather than discovered by the next reviewer. The residue is a table, **Completeness
registers and what actually bounds each**, naming per register the mechanism, the
residual hand-maintained input, and whether the claim is *complete* or *superset*.
No register may claim completeness unless its input is derived. That table is not
another turtle; it is the one that names each turtle.

**The orchestrator produced the same defect three times while enforcing it on
everyone else, which is the part worth remembering.** A ratification gate was
written as `grep -q 'OWNER-SIGNED'` while the comment explaining the gate
contained the string `OWNER-SIGNED` — it passed on its own documentation, ninety
seconds after a warning against exactly that. A harness run to check five
verification greps was executed with the `$ADR` variable unbound and reported
five false REDs, and the specs were briefed with that error as a documented
instance. And the cross-leg identifier collision that round 4 set out to remove
was *reinstated* by round 4's own fix, because the standing instruction never to
open the sibling spec — correct for write-safety during concurrent revision —
left neither leg able to verify an allocation against the other; leg 2 handled
this correctly by declining to claim anything about leg 1's identifiers, leg 1
made the claim, and it was false in four places. The repair is a rule about
authority rather than about care: **a document may state its own identifiers and
the date its allocation was checked, and may not assert what another document
uses; cross-document allocation is supplied by whoever can read both.** The
general form, which survives this epic: when a document says *this list is
complete*, ask what generates the list, then ask what generates that, and keep
asking until the answer is a digest over a whole region or an explicitly
registered canonical input. Every earlier stopping point is a claim, not a
mechanism. Extends ADR-0031.
